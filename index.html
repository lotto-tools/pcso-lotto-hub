    Tax and claiming quick answers

    How much tax is taken from a PCSO jackpot?

    Twenty percent (20%) final withholding tax on the gross prize if the prize is more than ₱10,000. A ₱100,000,000 jackpot pays ₱20,000,000 in tax and ₱80,000,000 in cash. Prizes of ₱10,000 or less are exempt under the TRAIN Law (Republic Act No. 10963).

    Is the 20% taken from the excess over ₱10,000 or from the whole prize?

    From the whole prize. Once a prize exceeds ₱10,000, the 20% applies to the full amount, so a ₱10,001 prize pays ₱2,000.20 in tax and ₱8,000.80 in cash. A ₱10,000 prize pays no tax at all — that one-peso difference is the cliff in this calculator.

    Where do I claim, and how long do I have?

    Up to ₱10,000: any authorized lotto outlet or PCSO branch. ₱10,001 to ₱300,000: a PCSO branch office or the Main Office. Above ₱300,000, including every jackpot: the PCSO Main Office at Sun Plaza, Shaw Boulevard corner Princeton Street, Mandaluyong City, in person. The ticket must be claimed within one year of the draw date, or the prize is forfeited to PCSO's charity fund.

    Does the calculator include the 1% agent commission?

    No. The 1% commission on a jackpot goes to the authorized PCSO agent that sold the winning ticket, paid by PCSO — it is not deducted from the winner's prize. The only deduction from your prize is the 20% final tax.

    This calculator is an information tool, not tax or legal advice. PCSO withholds the tax at payout and its computation is final; confirm current requirements with PCSO before claiming.
