3. Accuracy and source of the data

Draw results, jackpots and winner counts are taken from PCSO's published draw records, as mirrored in the open Lotto Tayo dataset (CC BY 4.0). We check each refresh for internal consistency — six numbers per draw, inside the game's pool, one draw per date, on the game's scheduled nights — and we state the coverage of our database on the pages that use it.

Even so, the site is provided “as is”. Data can be delayed, mis-transcribed upstream, or changed by PCSO. PCSO's official record prevails over anything shown here. Always confirm a win on PCSO's own publication or at a PCSO office before acting, and never rely on this site alone to decide whether a ticket won or how much it is worth.

4. Statistics and generated combinations

Every lottery draw is an independent random event. Frequencies, “hot” and “cold” numbers, overdue numbers, sums and pairs describe past draws only. They do not predict, and cannot improve, the chance of winning: those odds are fixed by the number of possible combinations (1 in 40,475,358 for Ultra Lotto 6/58, for example). The generator produces random combinations inside your chosen filters and is offered for entertainment and study.
