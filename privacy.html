Privacy

Privacy policy

This site is a set of static pages about PCSO lotto results and prize rules. It has no accounts, no forms that ask for personal details, and no ticket checking. This page explains the small amount of data that still exists when a site like this is used.

Last updated: November 16, 2026. This policy applies to lotto-tools.github.io/pcso-lotto-hub (the “site”). References to “we” and “us” mean the operator of this site.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, or store, your name, address, phone number, email address, ticket numbers, ID documents or payment details. The number board and the tax calculator run inside your browser: the values you type are not sent to us and are not saved anywhere.

We do not have a mailing list, we do not require registration, and we do not sell tickets or accept bets, so we never need a player account.

Information that is collected automatically

Like almost every website, our hosting provider records technical information when a page is requested — typically the IP address, the type of device and browser, the page requested, the time, and a referring address. This is used to serve the pages, keep the service secure and understand in aggregate how the site is used. Server logs are kept for a limited period and are not used to identify individual visitors.

We use no first-party advertising or tracking cookies of our own.

Cookies and advertising

If advertising is shown on this site, it is supplied by Google AdSense and its partners. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in the EU/UK, youronlinechoices.com.
  • Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy & Terms page.

Cookies set by third parties are controlled by those parties, not by us. Where consent is required by law, an advertising consent message is shown before non-essential cookies are set, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time.

Analytics

If we use a privacy-respecting analytics tool, it records aggregated page views and referrers without building a profile of you. Any analytics settings are limited to what is needed to know which pages are useful.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to PCSO's website or to the open dataset we use. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content or their handling of your data.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and lotto play is restricted to adults by PCSO.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to processing, or to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no account data, most requests concern server logs or advertising cookies set by third parties; where that is the case we will explain who holds the data and how to reach them. Write to the address below and we will answer within a reasonable period.

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may also contact the National Privacy Commission. Under the GDPR and similar laws you may contact your local supervisory authority.

Security and retention

The site is served over HTTPS. Data necessarily held for security and aggregate statistics is kept only as long as it serves that purpose, then deleted or anonymised.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be visible on this page.
